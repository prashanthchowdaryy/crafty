Questions We Are Often Asked

Where is Crafty Korner India located?

We are at Shilparamam Arts & Crafts Village, Madhapur, Hyderabad 500081. The shop is open 10:30 am to 8:00 pm, every day.

Do you deliver handmade wooden furniture and handicrafts across India?

Yes. We deliver across India, and we send photos and a short video on WhatsApp before dispatch so you can see the exact piece.

Yes. Send a reference photo, the size and your budget on WhatsApp or through our custom order page, and we reply with wood options, a price and a time.
