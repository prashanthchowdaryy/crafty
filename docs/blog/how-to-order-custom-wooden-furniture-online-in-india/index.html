How to order custom wooden furniture online in India

A simple guide to ordering a made-to-measure piece from a workshop you cannot visit.

05 November 2026 · 2 min read

Ordering furniture without visiting the workshop feels risky, but a few habits make it safe. This is the process we follow with customers across India.

Start with a photo and a size Send a reference photo and the measurements of the space. A rough sketch on paper is fine. The more exact the size, the fewer surprises later.

Decide the wood and finish Ask the maker to show you the wood in a short video, including the grain and the polish. Our [wood guide](/blog/teak-rosewood-or-sheesham-which-wood-for-handmade-furniture/) explains the common choices.

Agree the price and the time in writing A good quote lists the wood, the size, the finish, the delivery charge and the number of days. Keep it on WhatsApp or email so both sides can check it.

Pay in stages A fair arrangement is an advance to start the work and the balance after you have seen photos of the finished piece, before it is packed.

Check the packing Wooden furniture should be wrapped, padded at the corners and crated for long journeys. Ask how it will travel and who is responsible in transit.
