How to Care for Your Wooden Handicrafts and Keep Them Timeless

Simple, safe habits to keep carved, inlay and polished wood looking good for years.

09 November 2026 · 2 min read

Handmade wood ages well when it is looked after. A carved tray, an inlay box or a pair of stools can stay in good shape for many years with a few easy habits.

Dust gently and often Wipe the surface with a soft, dry cotton cloth. For carved patterns, use a soft brush, such as a clean paintbrush, to lift dust out of the grooves. Dust left in carving holds moisture and dulls the finish.

Keep it away from water and strong sun Wood swells with water and fades in strong sunlight. Keep pieces away from windows that get direct sun, from bathrooms, and from the path of a cooler or an AC vent. Use coasters and mats under cups, plants and hot dishes.

Polish a little, not often Once or twice a year, rub in a very small amount of a good wood wax or natural oil with a soft cloth, then buff it dry. Too much oil leaves the surface sticky and collects dust. Test any product on a hidden corner first.

Be careful with inlay and metal work Inlay in brass, metal or other materials is set into the wood by hand. Do not soak it or scrub it. Wipe with a barely damp cloth and dry the piece straight away.

Trays, boxes and goblets Wipe them clean after use and let them dry fully before you store them. Do not put wooden pieces in a dishwasher. If you plan to use a piece for food or drink, ask us about its finish before you buy.
